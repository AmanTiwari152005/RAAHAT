About RAAHAT

Students working together for education, awareness, and social good.

RAAHAT – Be The ChangeMaker is a social welfare club established in 2009 at Lakshmi Narain College of Technology.

We are a group of passionate students working to uplift underprivileged communities through education, awareness, and social initiatives.

What We Do

Small acts, organized with purpose.

From classrooms to communities, RAAHAT creates consistent opportunities for students to serve.

Teaching Sessions

T Lok Utthan sessions that support children with regular learning and mentoring.

Donation Drives

Collection and distribution efforts for essentials, books, clothes, and useful supplies.

Awareness Campaigns

Student-led campaigns that encourage healthier, safer, and more informed communities.

Khel Mela

One of RAAHAT's biggest events where 100+ kids play games, compete, and win exciting prizes.

Plantation Drives

Green drives that bring students together to protect and restore local environments.

Blood Donation Drives

Compassionate campaigns that connect willing donors with people who need support.
