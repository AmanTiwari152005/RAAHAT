About RAAHAT

Students working together for education, awareness, and social good.

RAAHAT – Be The ChangeMaker is a social welfare club established in 2009 at Lakshmi Narain College of Technology.

We are a group of passionate students working to uplift underprivileged communities through education, awareness, and social initiatives.

15+ years of student-led service Learning becomes powerful when it reaches everyone.

Featured Event

Khel Mela

One of RAAHAT's biggest events where 100+ kids participate in fun games, competitions, and win exciting prizes.

Khel Mela is one of the biggest events organized by RAAHAT – Be The ChangeMaker, where around 100+ children participate in exciting games, activities, and competitions.

The event is designed to encourage teamwork, confidence, joy, and active participation among children. Winners are rewarded with exciting prizes and gifts, creating memorable experiences and smiles for every participant.
